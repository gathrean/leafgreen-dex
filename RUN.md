# My LeafGreen run

Choices made so far, kept alongside the site. The same list shows at the top of the Plan tab.

## The run

- Pokémon LeafGreen. Rival Gary picked Charmander, so his last team ends with a Lv 63 Charizard.
- Starter Bulbasaur. Your roaming beast after the Sevii Islands will be Entei.
- Goal: catch them all, with a Straw Hat crew as the main team.

## The crew (party order = joining order)

- Luffy: Bulbasaur, Ivysaur, then Venusaur at Lv 32.
- Zoro: Dodrio, from Doduo on Route 16. Press B at Lv 31 to wait for Drill Peck at 37.
- Nami: Jolteon, from the Celadon Eevee (Lv 25) and a Thunder Stone.
- Usopp: Nidoking. Evolved with a Moon Stone before Lv 22, so Thrash at 22 and Megahorn at 43.
- Sanji: Ninetales. Keep Vulpix until Lv 29 for Flamethrower, then the Fire Stone.
- Nico Robin: Lapras, the Silph Co. 7F gift.

## Decisions made

- Fossil: Helix (Omanyte). Old Amber for Aerodactyl too.
- CH'DING (Farfetch'd, traded for Spearow) is the HM carrier: Cut and Fly. Keep his Stick on him.
- TM34 Shock Wave from Lt. Surge is reserved for Nami.
- TM28 Dig goes to Usopp for Lt. Surge.
- Mega Punch tutor (Route 4) was recommended for Usopp over Mega Kick.
- Luffy's Sleep Powder comes back at the Two Island Move Reminder: Big Mushroom from Pokémon Tower 5F, or two Tiny.
- Master Ball: save it for Mewtwo or Entei.
- Day Care: Nidoran♀ suggested, for Nidorina and then Nidoqueen with the spare Moon Stone.

## Stand-ins

- Butterfree: core until Zoro joins. Sleep Powder at Lv 15 for catching.
- Pikachu: benched.
- Pidgeotto “Bobby”: deposited.
- Skipped: Sandshrew, Magikarp training, the Paras mushroom hunt, the MR. NIDO trade.
